DROP YOUR PORTFOLIO FILES HERE
==============================
Anything in this folder stays on your computer only (it is git-ignored),
so it is safe to put your raw resume, notes, etc. here.

Helpful things to add:
  - profile photo                (any name, e.g. me.jpg)
  - resume / CV                  (PDF)
  - project photos               (put them in a subfolder per project if you like)
  - details.txt                  (anything you want on the site: college, years,
                                  internships, certifications, LinkedIn URL,
                                  extra projects, real skill levels, etc.)

Then tell Claude "I've uploaded the files" and it will place them on the site.
